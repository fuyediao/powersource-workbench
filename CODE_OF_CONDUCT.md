# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free collaboration environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy and professional respect.
- Welcome constructive feedback and different perspectives.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private credentials, customer information, and operational data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The owner may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the project and its contributors.
